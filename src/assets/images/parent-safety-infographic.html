⚠️ TOP 5 THREATS TO AFRICAN YOUTH
1. PREDATORS / GROOMING
What they do: Pretend to be teens, build "friendship," request intimate photos, threaten blackmail

Action: Screenshot everything, report to police immediately
2. SEXTORTION
What it is: Convince child to send intimate photos, then threaten to share unless they pay

Teach: "Once you send a photo, you lose control forever"
3. MOBILE MONEY SCAMS
Common: "Wrong transfer" scam, SIM swap, fake free data

Prevention: Register in YOUR name, set daily limits ₵500-1,000
4. CYBERBULLYING
Signs: Mean comments, threats, rumors, exclusion

Action: Screenshot, block, report to platform + school + police if severe
5. INAPPROPRIATE CONTENT
Exposure to: Violence, sexual content, dangerous challenges

Prevention: Enable Restricted Mode, weekly check-ins on what they watch
